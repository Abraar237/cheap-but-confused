Gemini 2.5 Flash (older, thinking on, 72 tasks) and Gemini 3.6 Flash (newest, 36 tasks) bracket the main model. External-trap accuracy under the append-only layout rises with generation, 40% to 66% to 80%, and under pruning it is 84%, 96% and 100%. The gap shrinks (27, 18, 11 points; label permutation p = 0.035 over all tasks, p = 0.092 on the 36 tasks common to all three) and does not close. The pointer note does more for the older model: on 2.5 Flash it recovers 79% of the gap and lifts external-trap accuracy from 40% to 75%. On every model pruning is the cheapest layout per correct episode, at the billed price and at every counterfactual cache-read price; on 3.6 Flash the implicit cache never serves the history at all, so its two layouts cost the same per episode.
